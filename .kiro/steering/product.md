# Product Steering

TruthBounty is a smart-contract protocol for decentralized claim verification, verifier staking, reputation-weighted participation, dispute resolution, and reward distribution.

The approved V2 deployment and execution topology targets Ethereum-compatible EVM networks, including Optimism. Stellar compatibility is planned only; it is not an active deployment target unless maintainers explicitly activate that work.

Security, deterministic accounting, auditable governance, and reliable release gates take precedence over convenience. Changes must preserve approved consumer interfaces and protocol behavior unless a separately reviewed requirement authorizes a change.
